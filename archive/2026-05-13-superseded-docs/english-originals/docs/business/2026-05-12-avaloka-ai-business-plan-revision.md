# Avaloka AI Business Plan Revision

Date: 2026-05-12

Status: User discovery stage

## 1. CEO Verdict

Avaloka AI should not be written as a broad "Buddhist wisdom AI" or "AI mental health product."

The business plan should be rewritten around a narrower, sharper wedge:

> Avaloka AI is a private wisdom companion for overseas Chinese women in midlife who face loneliness, childlessness, aging, illness anxiety, depression-adjacent low points, and death anxiety, and who want immediate compassionate grounding without religious recitation or generic advice.

Current evidence supports a **personal origin + user discovery stage**. It does not yet support a claim of validated market demand.

## 2. What Changed in the Business Thesis

### Old Thesis

Avaloka AI helps users solve psychological problems through Buddhist wisdom.

### Problem With the Old Thesis

This is too broad.

It sounds like:

- A therapy substitute.
- A Buddhist encyclopedia.
- A general AI companion.
- A spirituality content product.

That positioning creates safety risk, weak differentiation, and unclear monetization.

### New Thesis

Avaloka AI helps a specific first user group in a specific high-pain moment:

> Revised: When an overseas Chinese woman in midlife is alone at night, afraid of aging, illness, death, childlessness, or meaning collapse, Avaloka gives her a private compassionate-wisdom conversation that helps her stabilize, feel seen, and take one wise next step without reciting doctrine.

This is a stronger business thesis because it names:

- A specific user.
- A specific moment.
- A specific pain.
- A specific retention and continued-use signal.
- A specific product promise.

## 3. Current Demand Evidence

### Evidence Level

Current evidence is early but meaningful.

Avaloka has:

- 1 close first-user origin case from a real family member.
- 3 target users willing to try a rough MVP for 7 days.
- 2 target users who explicitly stated willingness to keep using if it genuinely helps.
- Repeated user language around privacy, wisdom feedback, night-time fear, and companionship that does not get tired of them.

Avaloka does not yet have:

- Actual 7-day usage data.
- Free retention beyond the first test.
- Retention.
- Referral behavior.
- Evidence from users outside the founder's close network.

### Commercially Important User Quotes

Use these in the business plan because they are specific:

- "I do not need more information input. I need wisdom feedback."
- "ChatGPT is like a smart stranger."
- "I need something that can understand the pain behind my words."
- "I do not want cold doctrine or generic comfort."
- "I need something absolutely private, wise, compassionate, and never tired of me."

## 4. Target Customer

### Initial ICP

ICP means ideal customer profile: the narrow first group the product should serve before expanding.

Avaloka's first ICP:

- Overseas Chinese woman.
- Age 45-60.
- Childless, DINK, single, widowed, empty-nest-like, or emotionally isolated.
- Has meditation, spiritual learning, contemplative practice, or interest in wisdom culture.
- Already spends time or money on books, courses, retreats, donations, therapy, coaching, meditation, or spiritual learning.
- Experiences night-time loneliness, aging fear, chronic illness anxiety, depression-adjacent low points, death anxiety, or meaning collapse.
- Feels that ordinary friends, generic AI, advice content, or clinical language do not fully understand her private pain.

### Do Not Start With

- "Everyone with stress."
- "All Buddhists."
- "All Chinese speakers."
- "People with mental health problems."
- "Anyone interested in wisdom culture."

Those are too broad to sell to.

## 5. Core Pain

The user does not lack information, advice, or spiritual content.

She may already consume:

- YouTube talks.
- Wisdom, spiritual, or psychology books.
- Online courses.
- Meditation, spiritual, or reflective communities.
- Friends or fellow practitioners.

The pain is:

> She cannot convert general wisdom, learning, or advice into a personal, compassionate response at the exact moment her pain arises.

More specifically:

- The pain appears at night.
- The pain is private and shame-bearing.
- The pain involves aging, illness, childlessness, death, and meaning.
- Existing people often respond with shallow comfort or cold doctrine.
- Existing AI responds correctly but often without warmth, courage, and human-feeling wisdom.

## 6. Product Wedge

Avaloka's first free validation product should not be the full companion vision.

The narrowest free validation wedge is:

> Night-time emotional settling through compassionate wisdom, without doctrine recitation.

Do not call it "emotional emergency" or "crisis support" in user-facing material. That sounds medical and creates safety expectations Avaloka should not carry.

Better names:

- 深夜安顿
- 夜间心灵陪伴
- 低谷时刻智慧陪伴
- 无常时刻陪伴
- 安心对话

## 7. What the MVP Should Do

The MVP should do one job extremely well:

> In a lonely or fearful night-time moment, Avaloka helps the user feel seen, responds with compassion and practical wisdom, and gives one immediate practice.

MVP flow:

1. User writes the painful situation.
2. Safety gate checks for self-harm or acute crisis.
3. Avaloka identifies the emotional pattern.
4. Avaloka retrieves a relevant wisdom passage or principle.
5. Avaloka responds with empathy, compassionate wisdom, and one small practice.
6. Avaloka stores a privacy-preserving summary if the user allows memory.

## 8. Medical and Depression Boundary

The business plan should include illness and depression-adjacent pain, but it must not claim to treat illness or depression.

Do not write:

> Avaloka solves physical disease and depression that modern medicine cannot cure.

Write:

> Many users who receive medical care, therapy, medication, or community support still face existential pain that those systems do not fully cover: loneliness, aging, illness fear, death anxiety, childlessness grief, and the question of how to make peace with this life. Avaloka does not replace medical or psychological care. It supports daily emotional settling, meaning-making, and private reflection outside clinical treatment.

Required boundary:

> Avaloka does not provide diagnosis, treatment, crisis counseling, or medical advice. If a user expresses self-harm, suicidal ideation, harm to others, or acute crisis, the system routes away from normal conversation and encourages immediate human or professional support.

## 8A. Buddhist Ethics Boundary

Avaloka should also state internally that every answer is guarded by the Five Mindfulness Trainings. This is the product's ethical operating system.

The user-facing promise:

> Avaloka's responses are guided by five hidden safety and wisdom commitments: respect for life, true happiness, true love, loving speech and deep listening, and nourishment and healing.

The business meaning:

- Avaloka will not encourage harm, revenge, despair, or dehumanization.
- Avaloka will not tell users that wealth, status, punishment, or control will solve suffering.
- Avaloka will not encourage dependency, coercion, or boundary-breaking in relationships.
- Avaloka will not use cold doctrine, shame, dismissal, or divisive speech.
- Avaloka will not encourage escapist consumption, spiritual bypassing, or rumination loops.

This matters because the target user is not asking for "more AI" or more religious language. She is looking for trust, steadiness, compassion, and wisdom. The Five Mindfulness Guardian gives Avaloka a hidden moral difference from generic chatbots.

## 9. Revenue Model

### Recommended Model

Do not test monetization in the first validation round.

This pain is recurring, but the first test should prove free retention before testing price. The product value comes from repeated access during unpredictable low moments.

### Test Price

Pricing test is deferred.

Do not ask users to pay during the first 7-day test.

Why defer payment:

- The current priority is to prove that users return without founder prompting.
- Payment questions can distort early emotional-safety feedback.
- If users do not want continued free access, payment does not matter.

### Future Packaging

Do not build pricing tiers in the MVP. Future pricing can be revisited only after free retention is proven.

- Possible future subscription.
- Possible donation-supported model.
- Possible paid guided reflection program.
- Possible local-first personal companion license.

### What Users Pay For

Users are not using Avaloka for:

- Generic AI chat.
- Buddhist encyclopedia answers.
- More videos or courses.
- Medical care.

Users return for:

- Privacy.
- Immediate availability.
- Compassionate wisdom without jargon.
- Non-judgmental emotional containment.
- Personalized interpretation of their specific life pain.
- A repeatable way to move from panic to one next step.

## 10. What Makes This a Successful Business

Avaloka becomes a successful business only if it proves all five conditions:

1. **High-pain moment usage**
   Users open it during real low moments, not only during curiosity or demos.

2. **Seven-day retention**
   Users come back across a week without being pushed by the founder.

3. **Continued-use pull**
   Users ask to keep using Avaloka after the free test.

4. **User language match**
   Users describe Avaloka as "懂我", "安顿", "有智慧", "不评判", or similar language, not "another chatbot" and not "满口佛言佛语."

5. **Safety trust**
   Users understand that Avaloka is not therapy, not medicine, and not crisis care.

6. **Five Mindfulness trust**
   Users can feel that answers are consistently protected by respect for life, loving speech, non-harming, non-exploitation, and nourishing practice.

## 11. First Validation Milestone

Avaloka should not measure success by downloads, signups, or compliments.

The first milestone:

> Recruit 5 target users. Get 3 to complete a 7-day free trial. Get 2 to ask for continued access. Get at least 1 to say she would recommend it to someone similar.

Minimum experiment:

- 5 users.
- 7 days.
- One daily check-in.
- One low-moment use case.
- End-of-trial continued-use interview.

Pass criteria:

- 3/5 use it at least 4 days out of 7.
- 2/5 ask for continued access.
- 1/5 says she would recommend it to someone similar.
- 0/5 experience safety confusion or believe it replaces therapy/medical care.

## 12. Changes Needed in the Business Plan

### Change 1: Rewrite the opening

Use:

> Avaloka AI is a private compassionate-wisdom companion for overseas Chinese women in midlife who face loneliness, aging, illness fear, childlessness grief, death anxiety, and meaning collapse.

Do not use:

> Avaloka AI is a private large model for Buddhist wisdom.

### Change 2: Replace "market is large" with "first user evidence"

Use:

> Early user discovery identified 3 target users willing to test for 7 days. The next validation step is to measure whether they return during real low moments and ask to keep using Avaloka after the free test.

Do not use:

> Many people have psychological problems and need wisdom culture.

### Change 3: Replace feature list with user job

Use:

> The job is deep night-time settling: helping users move from lonely panic, illness fear, or death anxiety into a calmer state through compassionate wisdom and one concrete practice.

Do not lead with:

- RAG.
- ChromaDB.
- Ollama.
- Prompt engineering.
- Local model.

Those are implementation details, not the business.

### Change 4: Add safety boundary

The plan must state that Avaloka does not treat depression, diagnose disease, or replace crisis support.

### Change 5: Add validation metrics

The next phase is not "build the whole app."

The next phase is:

- 5 users.
- 7 days.
- Free 7-day continued-use test.
- Retention and recommendation intent.
- Safety and tone feedback.

## 13. Revised One-Paragraph Business Summary

Avaloka AI is a private compassionate-wisdom companion for overseas Chinese women in midlife who experience loneliness, childlessness grief, aging fear, illness anxiety, death anxiety, and meaning collapse. These users often already study wisdom, spiritual, psychological, or contemplative content, but they lack immediate, personalized feedback when pain appears at night or during private low moments. Avaloka does not provide therapy, medical advice, or crisis counseling. It helps users feel seen, settle their nervous system, and take one concrete next step without reciting doctrine or filling the answer with religious language. The first wedge is a free 7-day test of "deep night-time settling" to measure real low-moment usage and continued-use pull.
